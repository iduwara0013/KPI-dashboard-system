## 7. New work, locks and changes

After a person has completed their only responsibility, assign another legitimate incomplete responsibility through their real supervisor (or create/publish a new activity with balanced weights, then assign it). Their automatic personal completion falls according to the new relative contribution weights; do not force equal weights. If an approved custom personal allocation exists, review/publish its updated 100% version.

Example: after engineer 17536 completes their 1.176%-contribution leaf, assign a new 3%-contribution activity directly from CIO (using an eligible reporting-tree employee assignment). Automatic completion is 1.176 / (1.176 + 3) = 28.16% until that new work is approved. Do not use an already allocated original activity twice; reassign it with the assignment controls or create new published scope. A reassignment can change the original holder’s scope and percentage too.

Started breakdowns stay locked. Editor may view them; evidence/approval history is preserved. Original plan changes are saved and published from Edit plan / add activities, followed by assignment review when source details change. Extra work remains separate unless the authorized replacement process is approved.
